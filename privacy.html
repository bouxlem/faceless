Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

faceless is a personal tool used to create and manage short-form social-media content.

Information processed

When you connect a supported social-media account, faceless may process authorization information and tokens required for permissions you grant. It may also process content and basic account information needed for the requested publishing workflow.

Use

Information is used to operate content creation, account connection, publishing, and related management functions.

Storage

Authentication credentials and tokens are intended to be stored locally and securely by the owner. Sensitive credentials should not be shared publicly.

Third-party services

Authorized integrations such as TikTok or YouTube may receive information necessary to perform the requested operation. Their privacy policies apply to their processing.

Data sharing

faceless does not intentionally sell personal information or share it with unrelated third parties except where necessary to operate an authorized integration or comply with law.

Revoking access

You can revoke integrations through the connected platform's account settings and remove locally stored credentials from the faceless configuration.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the service or integrations change.
